How it works

Three steps. Nothing to maintain.

No manual entry, no spreadsheet you'll abandon in a week. Recur does the reading; you do the deciding.

Connect a bank

Link your account in under two minutes through Mono, a licensed open banking provider. Recur never sees your login, only read-only access to transactions.

We find the pattern

Recur scans your history for charges that repeat: same merchant, close to the same amount, on a rough schedule. Two matches is enough to flag it.

You take it from there

See every recurring charge in one place, get a heads-up before the next one lands, and mark anything cancelled so Recur adjusts your total instantly.
