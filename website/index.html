The questions people actually ask.

Which banks does Recur work with?

Recur connects through Mono, so it reaches most Nigerian banks and the major neobanks: GTBank, Zenith, Access, First Bank, UBA, Kuda, Opay, Moniepoint and the rest. If Mono supports your bank, Recur does. You link the account in about two minutes.

How does Recur know a charge repeats?

It reads your history for charges from the same merchant, at close to the same amount, on a rough schedule. Two matches is enough to flag it, and a third confirms the cycle. Recur handles the fact that the amount moves, which is why anything billed in dollars still gets caught even though the naira figure changes every month.

Can Recur cancel a subscription for me?

No, and it will not pretend otherwise. Cancelling means acting on your account with the merchant, which Recur has no access to. What it does instead is tell you the exact steps for that specific merchant, and then watch whether the charge actually stops. If it does not, you will hear about it, which is the part people usually find out about a year late.

What if it labels something wrong?

Correct it once and every future charge from that merchant follows. The same applies in reverse: mark something as not recurring and Recur stops counting it in your total. Nigerian bank narrations are messy enough that getting some of them wrong is a certainty, so fixing them is one tap rather than a support ticket.

Can Recur move money out of my account?

No. The connection is read-only by design, and no transfer, payment or withdrawal capability exists in it. You authenticate on Mono’s own page, so Recur never sees or stores your bank login. You can unlink an account, or delete everything Recur holds on you, whenever you want.

What about free trials?

A trial leaves no transaction behind it, so there is nothing for Recur to find until the first charge lands, by which point you have already paid it. Log the trial once and Recur turns it into a deadline instead. It is the one thing on this page that asks something of you first.
