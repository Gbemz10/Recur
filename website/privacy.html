Privacy notice

Last updated 11 September 2026

Recur reads your bank transactions to find the charges that repeat. That is about as personal as data gets, so this page says plainly what we collect, why, who else touches it, and how to make us delete it.

The short version. We read your transactions, we never see your bank login, we cannot move your money, we do not sell anything to anyone, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

Who we are

Recur is operated by Damprose Innovations Limited, a company registered in Nigeria. For anything in this notice, write to support@recur.website.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, and a password stored as a one-way hash we cannot reverse
  • The name you choose, and a profile picture if you add one

Keeping your account safe

  • One-time codes, stored hashed, valid for ten minutes
  • Session tokens, stored hashed and revocable
  • A device identifier, your IP address and browser or device description, so we can tell you when something signs in from a device we have not seen before

Your bank connection

  • The bank's name, and the last digits of the account number. We never store the full number
  • An identifier that lets us ask Mono about that account

We never see your bank login. You sign in inside a screen hosted by Mono, a CBN-licensed open banking provider. Your credentials never reach us.

Your transactions

  • The date, amount, description and payee of transactions on the account you connected
  • What we work out from them: which charges repeat, how often, what they cost, and what is coming next
  • Budgets you set, categories you correct, and trials you log

What you tell us to send

  • Whether you want renewal reminders and how many days ahead
  • Whether you want the weekly digest

Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 we rely on:

  • Your consent for everything to do with your bank account, your transactions and the emails we send. You give it when you connect a bank and you can take it back at any time
  • Performing our contract with you for your account itself, since we cannot run an account without an email address and a password
  • Our legitimate interest in security for the device and IP details behind new-device alerts

Who else touches it

Only the companies that make the product work:

  • Mono, for the bank connection and transaction history
  • Supabase, which hosts our database
  • Render, which runs our servers
  • Vercel, which serves this website
  • Resend, which delivers our email. A reminder or digest names the subscription it is about, so Resend handles that detail too

Some of these process data outside Nigeria. We will only continue to use them on a footing the NDPA allows.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your data for advertising
  • We do not run advertising or third-party tracking in the app or on this site
  • We cannot move your money. Recur has no payment capability of any kind, so no transfer, payment or withdrawal is possible through it
  • We never ask for your bank password, PIN or OTP. If anything claiming to be Recur asks, it is not us

How long we keep it

  • Your account and transaction data, while your account exists. Deleting the account deletes it
  • One-time codes, ten minutes
  • Session tokens, up to thirty days, or until you sign out
  • Waitlist emails, until we open, or until you ask us to remove you

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or hand it over. Most of it you can do yourself:

  • Delete everything. In the app, delete your account. We ask for your password, unlink you from Mono, and remove your data: transactions, detected subscriptions, budgets, sessions, all of it
  • Stop the bank connection. Unlink the account in the app and we stop reading anything new
  • Stop the emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and the settings screen has a switch for each kind

For anything else, write to support@recur.website. If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

How we protect it

  • Passwords and one-time codes are hashed with argon2id and cannot be read back
  • Sign-in tokens are short-lived and can be revoked
  • Secrets we must store are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM
  • Everything travels over HTTPS
  • Your bank credentials never reach us at all

Children

Recur is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is one that matters.
